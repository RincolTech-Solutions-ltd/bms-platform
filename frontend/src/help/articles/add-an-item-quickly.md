Quick add puts a new item, its prices and how many you have in one form. One tap saves them all.

1. Open **Quick add** under Stock in the menu. On a phone it is under **More**. A shop with no items yet also shows **Quick add an item** on Home and Sell.
2. Type the **Name** and the **Selling price**. The **Buying price** is optional. Type **How many do you have here now?** and check **Where**: it starts with the branch you are in, and you can pick a store.
3. Choose the group in **Category**, right under the name. It is needed, and the group you chose last time at this shop is chosen for you. No group fits? Choose + Add a group at the foot of the list, type its name and tap Add group: it is chosen and what you typed stays. The unit starts as a piece. Buy or sell it in cartons, boxes or other packages? Answer Yes to **Do you buy or sell it in boxes, cartons or other packages?** and add each package as on Add an item: the package, how many pieces (or smaller packages) one holds, and what it is used for. Then **How do you usually buy it, and for how much?**: choose Carton and type what one carton costs, and BMS works out the price of one piece. **How many do you have here now?** then takes the full cartons and the loose pieces. Add a **Photo** if you like.
4. Tap **Add**. BMS saves the item, its packages and its stock together and says what it added, such as "Test bulb added: 600 pc at Test Store", or "Test bulb added: 3 cartons and 5 pieces at Test Shop One". Tap **Add another** for the next item, or **Sell it** to put it on a sale.

> Already have an item with that name? Tap **Use the existing one and just add stock**. The number you typed is added to the stock it already has there: 50 and 10 more make 60. Other items are never changed.

> Just bought the goods with money from the shop? Tick **I just bought these, take the money from cash**, type the buying price, and choose Cash or Mobile money: the total comes out of that branch's money, as Buy stock does. Leave it off for goods you already had. For a bigger delivery or one from a supplier, use Buy stock in the menu.

> Quick add needs the internet. Without it, connect, then tap **Add** again.
