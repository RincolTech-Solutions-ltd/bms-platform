BMS works in your phone's browser. Installed, it opens like any other app from its icon, starts faster and can tell you when something needs you.

1. Find the card **Install the app**. It shows on the sign-in page, and always under **Notifications** in the menu.
2. On an Android phone or a computer, tap **Install** (on the Notifications page, **Install the app**) and confirm. If your browser did not offer it, open its menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. On an iPhone or iPad, use **Safari** in a normal tab, not a Private one. Tap **Install** to see the taps: tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open BMS from its new icon on your home screen.

> If you do not see Share, tap the address bar once so the buttons show. On an iPad, Share is at the top right.

> **Not now** hides the card on this phone. The Notifications page still has it.
