A person's role decides what they can see and do, and the ticked shops and stores decide where. An administrator changes both on the Staff page.

1. Open **Staff** under Business in the menu, find the person and tap **Change roles** on their card.
   ![Tap Change roles](change-roles-1.webp)
2. Pick the role, then under **Where does this person work?** tick each shop and store they work in, or every one.
   ![Tick where they work](change-roles-2.webp)
3. Tap **Save the roles**. The change counts from their next screen; they do not need a new link.
   ![Tap Save the roles](change-roles-3.webp)

> Need two jobs, such as Cashier in one shop and Storekeeper in a store? Tap **Add another role**.
